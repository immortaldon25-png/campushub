Returns and refunds

Last updated: 27 October 2026

CampusHub is a marketplace where students sell digital goods (such as notes, templates and designs) and services (such as tutoring, data entry and design work). Sellers are independent. [add BUSINESS_NAME in config.js] runs the platform and the payment protection.

The short version: your payment is held safely until you confirm you are happy. If something is wrong, open a dispute before you confirm and the money stays held while we review it.

How payment protection works

  • You and the seller agree the price in the CampusHub chat and you create an order.
  • You pay. The money is held and is not given to the seller yet.
  • The seller delivers inside the chat and marks the order delivered.
  • You check the delivery. If you are happy, tap Release payment. The seller is then paid.
  • If you are not happy, tap Open dispute. We review the chat and decide whether to refund you or pay the seller.

When you can get a refund

  • The seller did not deliver what was agreed, or did not deliver at all.
  • What you received is clearly different from the listing or from what was agreed in the chat.
  • The seller agrees to a refund before delivery. The seller can do this from the order panel.
  • You were charged twice or without your permission. Contact us straight away.

When a refund is not available

  • After you have confirmed and released the payment.
  • You changed your mind after receiving a digital product or service that matched the listing.
  • The order was cancelled before payment. Nothing was charged, so there is nothing to refund.

How to ask for a refund

  1. Open the order in your Inbox and tap Open dispute. Do this before you release payment.
  2. Explain the problem in the chat. Keep all messages inside CampusHub. Messages outside the app cannot be used as evidence.
  3. We review the chat and the delivered files and decide. We aim to reply within 5 working days.

When you will receive your money back

Approved refunds are returned to the card or bank account you paid with, through Paystack. Banks can take several working days to show the money. If you have not received it after 10 working days, contact us at [add CONTACT_EMAIL in config.js] or [add CONTACT_PHONE in config.js].

Seller unresponsive or buyer unresponsive

If the other person stops replying after payment, use the Report button in the chat or contact us. We review the evidence and may refund or release the payment. We do not release a seller's payment without the buyer's confirmation unless we have reviewed a dispute or a report.

Card chargebacks

Please contact us first. We can usually fix problems faster than a chargeback. Chargebacks made without contacting us may result in account suspension.
