Delivery policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

CampusHub supports digital goods and services only. We do not ship physical items, and physical goods may not be listed.

How digital goods are delivered

  • After you pay, the seller sends your files in the CampusHub chat. Files are private to you and the seller.
  • Accepted files: PDF, Word, PowerPoint, Excel, text and common image files, up to 10 MB each.
  • Sellers can only send files after payment is held. This protects both of you.
  • You can download your files from the chat at any time.

How services are delivered

Services (for example tutoring, design or data entry) are delivered as agreed between you and the seller in the chat. Agree what will be delivered and when before you pay. The seller delivers inside the chat, or by sharing the finished work as a file, and then marks the order delivered.

How long delivery takes

Digital files are normally sent soon after payment is held. For services, the time is agreed in the chat and shown by the seller. If the seller does not deliver on time, tell them in the chat. If they still do not deliver, open a dispute for a refund as described in our Returns and refunds policy.

After delivery

When the seller marks the order delivered, check it carefully. Release payment if you are happy, or open a dispute if you are not. Your payment is not released to the seller until you confirm, or until we have reviewed a dispute.

Delivery problems

  • You cannot open a file: tell the seller in the chat and ask for another format.
  • The file is wrong or incomplete: say so in the chat and open a dispute if it is not fixed.
