Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

[add BUSINESS_NAME in config.js] (RC [add RC_NUMBER in config.js]) is responsible for your personal data. This policy explains what we collect and why, in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act.

What we collect

  • Account: email, username, password (stored by our login provider in protected form).
  • Marketplace: your listings, photos, chat messages, files you send, orders and payment status.
  • Identity check: your name, and the result of the check. Your BVN and bank account details are sent straight to Paystack to confirm who you are. We do not store your BVN. We keep your verified name and the last 4 digits of your payout account.
  • Payout account: bank name, account name, last 4 digits and a payment reference from Paystack.
  • Study and AI tools: notes you type are kept on your device. Text you send to Jeevanom-IQ is sent to a third-party AI provider to produce an answer.
  • Safety: records of blocked messages and reports.

Why we use it

To run your account, show listings, deliver messages, process and protect payments, check identity, prevent fraud and abuse, answer disputes and support requests, and meet legal duties.

Who we share it with

  • Supabase hosts our database, login and file storage. Our project is hosted outside Nigeria.
  • Paystack processes payments, refunds, payouts and identity checks.
  • An AI provider processes Jeevanom-IQ questions.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal data.

Messages are screened

Chats, listings and file names are scanned automatically for contact details to keep trades inside the app. A person may read messages when a chat is reported or disputed.

How long we keep it

We keep account data while your account is active. We keep order and payment records for as long as the law requires, and no longer than needed.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its use, or to withdraw consent, by contacting [add CONTACT_EMAIL in config.js]. You can also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Security

We use encrypted connections, access rules on every table, private file storage, and keep secret keys on the server. No system is perfectly secure.

Age

CampusHub is for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from children. Contact us if you think a child has given us data and we will delete it.
